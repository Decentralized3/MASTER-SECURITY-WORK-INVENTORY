# Digital Forensics

## Security Blue Team — Digital Forensics

Digital-forensics training/certification is part of the professional background and is associated with 2024 in the recovered history.

### Areas studied

- digital evidence
- system forensics
- forensic investigation methodology
- artifact analysis
- network/forensic analysis

### Tools discussed in the broader study

Autopsy, Sleuth Kit, Guymager, dd/dc3dd, Plaso, Volatility 3, ExifTool, YARA, Wireshark, KAPE and the Eric Zimmerman toolset.

### Important distinction

The later toolchain research does **not** mean every tool listed above was independently used in a completed forensic case.

The public record should therefore describe the training and study accurately rather than claiming a completed investigation for every tool.
