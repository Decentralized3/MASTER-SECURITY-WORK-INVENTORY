# Publication Checklist

Before publishing an entry:

- [ ] Is the work real and supported by evidence?
- [ ] Is it clear what was actually done versus discussed?
- [ ] Are observations separated from confirmed vulnerabilities?
- [ ] Are failed/incomplete parts labelled honestly?
- [ ] Are target/program disclosure rules checked?
- [ ] Are secrets, tokens, cookies and personal data removed?
- [ ] Are assistant/AI-generated suggestions distinguished from user-executed work where relevant?
- [ ] Are dates and outcomes supported?
- [ ] Is the entry useful to a technical reader without unnecessary operational detail?
- [ ] Does the wording demonstrate the work rather than inflate it?
